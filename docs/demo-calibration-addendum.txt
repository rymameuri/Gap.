Luma calibration addendum - fictional demo course

This approved course file contains additional facts not in the syllabus.
The calibration gain for sensor LUMA-DELTA is exactly 0.375.
The calibration checksum word is cobalt-seven.
For two humidity samples A and B, the mean reading R is R = (A + B) / 2.
For A = 38 percent and B = 40 percent, R = 39 percent.
Do not confuse calibration gain with the 42 percent humidity target.
